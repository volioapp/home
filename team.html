02 · Why we built it

Aviation deserved better than a group chat

The flying happens, and then it vanishes. A screenshot here, a photo there, a story told once at the bar and never again.

It starts with the flight

Type a registration and the flight is there, drawn and measured. The part nobody enjoys doing by hand happens on its own.

It works because of people

Instructors, students, crew, spotters, the person in seat 14A on a day off. Aviation is small, and it should feel that way.

It is still early

We build with the community, not at it. What our users ask for is what gets made next.
