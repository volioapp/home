About Jester Aviation Academy

Jester Aviation Academy, also known as Jester Flight Dispatch School, trains people for the operational side of aviation. Its flight dispatcher course was developed by a working dispatcher and follows ICAO Doc 10106, mixing theory and practical lessons.

The school also prepares candidates for the Test of English for Aviation (TEA), runs airport security courses for companies, and advises operators setting up a flight operations department. Its operational office is at Milan Malpensa Airport.
