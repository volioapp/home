About ESA – English School for Aviation

E.S.A. helps pilots and aspiring pilots reach the English level their licence and their career need. Lessons are one to one and online, and are taught by airline pilots with international experience.

Courses cover ICAO English exam preparation, general English for an aviation career, preparation for airline pilot assessments, and a course on FAA terminology and communication for training in the United States. Students can also book the ICAO exam online through partner examiners.
