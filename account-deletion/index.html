Account Deletion Request

You can delete your Volio account at any time. Choose the method that works for you.

Delete directly from the app

The quickest way is directly in the Volio app: go to Profile → Delete Account. No email or support needed. If you signed in with Apple, your Apple credentials will also be revoked.

Don't have the app? Use this form

If the form doesn't load, open it directly at this link, or write to support@volioapp.it.

What gets deleted

Account data Profile, email, username, settings
Flight data Logged flights, GPS tracks, statistics linked to your account
Social content Posts, photos, comments, follower/following connections
Apple Sign-in If you signed in with Apple, the associated credentials are also revoked upon deletion

⚠ Note

Some data may be retained longer if required by legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory obligations, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
